Senior Backend Engineer (Platform)

About the role
We are looking for a senior backend engineer to join the platform team. You will
own services that process millions of events per day and mentor engineers across
two squads.

Requirements
- 6+ years of professional software engineering experience.
- Strong Python, with production experience in Django or FastAPI.
- Deep knowledge of PostgreSQL and query tuning.
- Experience running services on Kubernetes in AWS.
- Comfortable with CI/CD pipelines, observability and on-call rotations.
- Practical experience designing microservices and REST APIs.

Nice to have
- Go or Rust for performance-sensitive components.
- Kafka or another event streaming platform.
- Terraform for infrastructure as code.
- Experience mentoring junior engineers.
